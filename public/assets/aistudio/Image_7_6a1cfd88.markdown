## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the heartwarming honesty of wholesome, home-cooked food while delivering the operational clarity of a modern daily meal management service. It bridges emotional warmth‚Äîreminiscent of motherly affection, aromatic spice boxes, and simmering handis‚Äîwith the rigorous efficiency needed for dispatching hundreds of lunch and dinner tiffins on strict schedules.

### Visual Style
The aesthetic fuses **Warm Artisanal Minimalism** with **Functional Tactile Elements**:
- **Earthy and Appetizing:** Inspired by sun-baked terracotta clay vessels, glowing golden turmeric, aromatic saffron threads, and garden-fresh coriander or sage leaves.
- **Operational Clarity:** High-contrast information hierarchy, legible status counters, and glanceable operation cards ensure operators and customers can confirm meal schedules instantly without cognitive friction.
- **Crafted Warmth:** Softened geometries, subtle ambient warmth in surfaces, and clean borders replace cold sterile corporate patterns with inviting domestic comfort.

### Target Audience & Emotional Response
- **Subscribers & Diners:** Students, office-goers, and families yearning for reliable, non-greasy home food. The UI evokes comfort, hygiene, reliability, and heartfelt care.
- **Kitchen Operators & Dispatchers:** Home chefs and coordinators tracking meal confirmations (Lunch/Dinner: Yes/No counts). The UI provides dependable contrast, crisp typography, and stress-free data ingestion under morning rush hours.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the spice rack, brass tableware, and clay pots of a bustling home kitchen.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#C84B26` - Terracotta Clay):** Represents the hearth, tandoor heat, and slow-simmered gravies. Used for primary actions, critical meal confirmation indicators, and brand highlights.
- **Secondary (`#E08A1E` - Turmeric Amber / Saffron):** Symbolizes purity, warmth, and vitality. Used for active states, pending deliveries, meal streak celebrations, and live highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#3D7053` - Fresh Sage / Coriander):** Represents freshly plucked herbs, vegetarian purity, positive confirmations, and healthy nutrition metrics.
- **Neutral Core (`#241E1A` - Roasted Peppercorn):** Deep, warm charcoal-brown providing extreme legibility without the harshness of pure black.

### Surface Palette
- **Canvas Base (`#FCF9F5` - Warm Buttermilk Cream):** Soft, non-glare, comforting background surface that reduces eye fatigue during prolonged operational use.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` - Crisp Muslin White):** Clean, hygienic, bright white surfaces for metric cards, order lists, and actionable modules.
- **Surface Muted (`#F3ECE3` - Steamed Flour):** Neutral tonal background for nested blocks, table headers, and disabled controls.
- **Border Tone (`#E6DDD3` - Glazed Stoneware):** Subtle, grounding border lines that define cards and input fields without harsh visual noise.

## Typography

The type system relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to achieve a balance of geometric clarity, high legibility, and humanist friendliness.

- **Numerics & Operational Metrics:** `metric-number` is weighted at `800` to anchor operational boards‚Äîsuch as total active lunch plates or pending delivery tallies‚Äîmaking them visible across kitchen prep counters.
- **Editorial Headlines:** `headline-lg` and `headline-md` use tighter letter spacing to create an inviting, artisanal restaurant feel without losing digital precision.
- **Form Controls & Badges:** `label-md` and `label-sm` use bold weights and subtle tracking (`0.02em` to `0.04em`) to ensure tags like "Veg Deluxe", "Lunch Opted Out", and "Rank #1" remain crisp at glance speed.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model utilizes a fluid 12-column responsive grid on desktop and tablet, shifting to a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1rem` (`16px`), `gutter: 1rem` (`16px`). Metric cards stack or roll horizontally. Tiffin Yes/No switches occupy full width for one-hand thumb tap access.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, `margin: 1.5rem` (`24px`), `gutter: 1.25rem` (`20px`). Kitchen metrics display in a 2x2 grid.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, max-width `1280px` centered canvas, `margin-desktop: 2rem` (`32px`), `gutter-desktop: 1.5rem` (`24px`). Dashboard splits between the real-time operational monitor (7 columns) and menu broadcast / leaderboard feed (5 columns).

### Spacing Philosophy
Rhythm is built upon a consistent `4px / 8px` base unit.
- `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px): Used inside compact controls, badge padding, and icon-to-label offsets.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard internal card padding and form field vertical gaps.
- `space-lg` (24px) and `space-xl` (32px): Separation between distinct modules, such as separating the Live Order Counters from the Daily Menu Card.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid cold, hyper-digital interfaces, elevation relies on **ambient warm illumination** and **tactile stoneware layering**.

### Elevation Tiers
1. **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background tone `#FCF9F5`. Completely flat, non-reflective.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Module Resting):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface framed by a 1px border of `#E6DDD3` with a soft terracotta-tinted shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(36, 30, 26, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(200, 75, 38, 0.03)`.
3. **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Hover / Selected Cards):** Lifted cards (such as the active Daily Special or hovered meal slot): `0 8px 20px -4px rgba(36, 30, 26, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(200, 75, 38, 0.06)`.
4. **Level 3 (Broadcast Modals & Sliders):** High-priority overlays, live dispatch alerts, and confirm popups: `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(36, 30, 26, 0.16)`.

Borders retain an artisanal feel: crisp 1px strokes tinted with warm sand (`#E6DDD3`) ground every card so depth never feels floaty or unmoored.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded (`2`)** shape geometry, pairing approachable hospitality curves with structural balance.

### Corner Radius System
- **Base Components (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used for input fields, segment buttons, table rows, and small pill status tags.
- **Card Containers (`1rem` / `16px`):** Used for metric cards, menu broadcast cards, leaderboard modules, and dispatch tickets.
- **Modals & Hero Modules (`1.5rem` / `24px`):** Used for large featured daily special banners and mobile bottom sheets.
- **Pill Shape (`9999px`):** Strictly reserved for meal status chips (e.g., "Veg", "Non-Veg", "Confirmed", "Skipped") and toggle controls.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button (Order / Broadcast / Action):** Solid Terracotta (`#C84B26`) fill, pure white text, font `label-lg`. Padding: `12px 20px`. Hover brings a deep clay tone (`#B03F1E`) with subtle Level 2 shadow.
- **Secondary Button:** Warm Sand tint (`#F3ECE3`) background with Peppercorn (`#241E1A`) text and no border. Hover deepens to `#E8DFC9`.
- **Outline Button:** Transparent background, 1.5px solid `#C84B26` border, `#C84B26` text. Used for secondary administrative tasks like "Edit Add-on".
- **Affirmative Toggle Button (Yes/No):** Split rounded segment. "Yes" active uses fresh Sage Green (`#3D7053`) with white text. "No / Skip" active uses Muted Rust (`#D65C45`) tint with white text. Inactive side uses neutral light cream (`#F3ECE3`) with muted charcoal text.

### Metric Cards & Live Badges
- **Operational Metric Cards:** Crisp white cards featuring a 2-line layout: `metric-number` (36px, 800 weight) with an adjacent directional badge (+12% vs yesterday), anchored by a clean `label-md` description beneath.
- **Lunch / Dinner Live Badges:** Pill-shaped counters featuring a blinking 6px status dot.
  - Active Lunch: Turmeric Amber (`#E08A1E`) light tint (`#FEF6E9`) with dark amber border and bold text.
  - Active Dinner: Terracotta (`#C84B26`) light tint (`#FAECE8`) with terracotta border and bold text.

### Menu Broadcast Card
- A distinct highlighted card surfaced in warm parchment (`#FFFDF9`) framed by a subtle 1.5px border of `#E08A1E`.
- **Header:** Features a "Today's Tiffin" icon, chef's quote tag, and instant single-click WhatsApp/SMS "Broadcast Menu" trigger button.
- **Content Blocks:** Segregated rows for *Dal / Gravy*, *Sabzi / Dry*, *Breads (Roti / Paratha)*, and *Dessert / Salad*, each accompanied by warm pictorial tags.

### Customer Ranking & Streaks Leaderboard
- Card rows styled with alternating faint cream surfaces (`#FFFFFF` and `#FAF6F0`).
- **Rank Indicator:** Gold medal icon for #1 with Turmeric Amber accent, Silver/Bronze badges for #2 and #3.
